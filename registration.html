NSS Registration Guide 2024/2025

Follow this step-by-step guide to complete your National Service registration successfully in the new NSS system.

Go to NSS Portal

How to Register for National Service

The new NSS Online Registration System allows all eligible graduates to register, verify their details, and manage their postings online. Follow the guide below carefully to ensure your registration is successful.

Step 1: Access the NSS Registration Portal

Visit the official National Service Scheme portal at https://portal.nss.gov.gh. You will see options for “Enroll”, “Login”, and “Check Posting”.

Step 2: Create Your NSS Account

Click on “Enroll” to begin. Fill in your personal details exactly as they appear on your National ID or Ghana Card. You’ll also need your Index Number or Student ID for verification.

Step 3: Generate Your NSS PIN Code

Visit any regional or campus branch of the Ghana Commercial Bank (GCB) or use the MTN Mobile Money NSS PIN service to pay for your registration PIN code (GHS 40).

After payment, go back to the portal and enter your PIN to continue registration.

Step 4: Complete Online Registration

Provide accurate information such as your:

  • Full name and contact details
  • Educational background (Institution, Program, Index No.)
  • Preferred region of service
  • Emergency contact and next of kin

Step 5: Upload Required Documents

Upload the following documents in clear, scanned formats:

  • Passport-size photograph
  • National ID / Ghana Card
  • School ID or Transcript

Step 6: Submit & Print Your Registration Slip

After completing the form, review your details and click Submit. Then, download and print your NSS Registration Slip — you’ll need it during orientation and posting verification.

💡 Pro Tip: Always double-check your NSS PIN and details before final submission. Incorrect information may delay your posting.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What if I lost my NSS PIN?

You can retrieve your NSS PIN by visiting the NSS portal and clicking “Retrieve PIN”. Follow the instructions using your index number or email.

2. Can I change my preferred region after registration?

No. Once your form is submitted, changes are only possible through official NSS regional offices.

3. When does registration close?

Registration for the 2024/2025 service year closes on November 30, 2024. Late applications may not be processed.

4. Who can I contact for help?

For any issues, contact NSS Support via email at info@nss.gov.gh or call +233 30 277 7793.
