Monthly Allowance Payment Schedule

Posted on: September 15, 2023

The payment schedule for monthly allowances has been released. Service personnel should expect payments between the 25th and 30th of each month. Any delays should be reported to your regional coordinator.

Please ensure your bank details are updated and accurate in the NSS portal to avoid payment issues.
