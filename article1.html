2024/2025 National Service Registration Opens

Posted on: October 15, 2023

Registration Status The online registration for the 2025/2026 National Service year is closed. The deadline was extended to Friday, October 24, 2025. Key Updates and Announcements Service Year Commencement: The 2025/2026 service year officially began on Monday, November 3, 2025, and personnel were expected to report to their assigned posts on that day. Postings Released: The first batch of postings for over 85,000 graduates has been released. Personnel can check their placements on the official website, print their appointment letters, and book appointments for regional validation. New Digital Platform: The National Service Authority (NSA) introduced a new, more secure digital platform (Central Service Management Platform) to enhance transparency and security. Previous Registrations Invalidated: All registrations conducted in June 2025 were declared null and void due to data integrity concerns and discrepancies found during a forensic audit. Affected individuals had to re-register on the new portal and will receive refunds for payments made in June. Regional Validation: Regional validation and registration exercises were scheduled to take place from Wednesday, October 29, to Friday, November 21, 2025, at designated centres. Important Reminders for Personnel Official Website: All transactions and information should be accessed only via the official website: www.gnsa.gov.gh. No Middlemen: The NSA has strictly cautioned personnel against engaging the services of individuals or groups (scam syndicates) claiming they can influence postings or help with the process. Required Documents: Personnel must present their endorsed appointment letters and Ghana Cards at the point of registration and validation. Optional Military Training: An optional six-week military training program was announced for interested personnel to instil discipline and equip them with additional skills. The current focus is on personnel reporting to duty and completing their regional validation, as the registration period has concluded.

The system has been enhanced to make registration more efficient and user-friendly. Applicants are urged to verify their personal information before submission.
