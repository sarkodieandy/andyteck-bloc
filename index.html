Privacy Policy

PayCycle Tracker

Effective Date: January 2026

PayCycle Tracker is a utility app designed to help users manage loans, budgets, and personal financial records. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled within the app.

1. Information We Collect

PayCycle Tracker does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data entered into the app is stored locally on your device only.

The app may store the following locally on your phone:

  • Loan records you create
  • Budget and expense information
  • Payment schedules and reminders
  • App preferences and settings

2. How Your Data Is Used

All data is used solely to provide the app’s core functionality, such as tracking loans, managing budgets, and displaying summaries.

We do not analyze, share, sell, or upload your data.

3. Data Storage

All information is stored locally on your device using secure system storage. We do not use cloud storage, external databases, or third-party servers.

4. Data Sharing

PayCycle Tracker does not share any data with third parties. No data leaves your device.

5. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate third-party analytics, tracking tools, or advertising services that collect personal data.

6. User Control

You have full control over your data. You can edit or delete your records at any time directly within the app.

Uninstalling the app will permanently remove all locally stored data.

7. Children’s Privacy

PayCycle Tracker does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Security

We rely on your device’s built-in security features to protect locally stored data. However, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app or on this page.
