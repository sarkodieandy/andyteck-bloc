Welcome to DateHive! We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe, secure experience while using our dating platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web services.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide to Us

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Register an account: Email address, password, full name
  • Create your profile: Display name, date of birth, gender, sexual orientation preferences, location (city/country), biographical information, interests and hobbies
  • Upload content: Profile photos and images
  • Use our services: Messages sent to other users, likes and swipes, match preferences, reported content and safety concerns
  • Make purchases: Payment information (processed securely through Paystack), subscription details, billing history
  • Contact us: Any information you provide when contacting our support team

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use DateHive, we may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, mobile network information
  • Usage Information: App features you use, time spent on the app, profiles you view, actions taken (likes, passes, super likes)
  • Location Information: With your permission, we collect precise or approximate location data to show you matches in your area
  • Log Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, time and date of visits, error logs

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Social Media: If you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive basic profile information (name, email, profile picture) as permitted by your privacy settings
  • Payment Processors: Payment confirmation and transaction data from Paystack (we do not store your full credit card details)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

2.1 Provide and Improve Our Services

  • Create and manage your account
  • Show your profile to potential matches based on preferences
  • Enable communication between matched users
  • Process your subscription payments and manage billing
  • Personalize your experience and show relevant matches
  • Improve our matching algorithm and app features
  • Provide customer support and respond to your inquiries

2.2 Safety and Security

  • Verify user identities and prevent fraud
  • Detect and prevent abuse, harassment, and violations of our Terms of Service
  • Investigate and respond to reports of inappropriate behavior
  • Protect the safety and security of our users
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our policies

2.3 Communications

  • Send you important updates about our services
  • Notify you about matches, messages, and app activity
  • Send promotional offers and premium subscription information (you can opt-out)
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and requests

2.4 Analytics and Research

  • Analyze usage patterns and user behavior
  • Conduct research to improve our services
  • Generate aggregated, non-identifiable statistics

3. How We Share Your Information

3.1 With Other Users

Your profile information (photos, display name, age, location, bio, interests) is visible to other DateHive users based on your match preferences. When you match with someone, they can see your full profile and communicate with you.

3.2 With Service Providers

We share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate DateHive:

  • Supabase: Database hosting, authentication, and backend infrastructure
  • Paystack: Payment processing for premium subscriptions
  • Firebase: Push notifications and cloud messaging
  • Cloud Storage: Secure storage of user photos and content

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

3.3 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose your information if required by law or if we believe it's necessary to:

  • Comply with legal processes, court orders, or government requests
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and other agreements
  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of DateHive, our users, or the public
  • Investigate fraud, security issues, or violations of our policies

3.4 Business Transfers

If DateHive is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

3.5 With Your Consent

We may share your information for other purposes with your explicit consent.

⚠️ Important: We will NEVER sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Your privacy is our priority.

4. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

4.1 Access and Update Your Information

You can access and update your profile information at any time through the app settings:

  • Edit your profile details, photos, bio, and interests
  • Update your match preferences and location
  • Change your email address and password

4.2 Delete Your Information

You have the right to delete your account and personal information:

  • Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
  • Your profile will be permanently removed from DateHive
  • Your messages, matches, and activity will be deleted
  • Some information may be retained for legal compliance (e.g., transaction records for 7 years)

4.3 Control Your Visibility

  • Pause your profile to temporarily hide from other users
  • Block specific users to prevent them from seeing your profile or contacting you
  • Report inappropriate behavior or content

4.4 Location Data

  • You can enable or disable location services in your device settings
  • Without location data, you can still use DateHive but matching may be less accurate
  • You can manually update your location in the app

4.5 Marketing Communications

  • Opt-out of promotional emails by clicking "unsubscribe" in any marketing email
  • Manage push notification preferences in app settings
  • You will still receive important service-related communications

4.6 Data Portability

You can request a copy of your personal data in a portable format. Contact us at privacy@datehive.com to request your data.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest
  • Secure Authentication: Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls limit who can view your data
  • Regular Audits: We regularly review and update our security practices
  • Payment Security: Payment data is processed by PCI-DSS compliant providers (Paystack)
  • Monitoring: 24/7 monitoring for suspicious activity and security threats

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations:

  • Active Accounts: Data is retained while your account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: Most data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion
  • Legal Compliance: Some data (e.g., payment records, safety reports) may be retained longer to comply with legal requirements
  • Backup Systems: Data may persist in backup systems for up to 90 days after deletion
  • Aggregated Data: Anonymized, aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for analytics
