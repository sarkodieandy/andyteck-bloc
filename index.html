Privacy Policy — Habit Tracker AI

Last updated: November 14, 2025

Thank you for using Habit Tracker AI (the “App”), provided by Terlo Service (“we”, “us”, “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information. Except where required for advertising services, we do not collect or store your personal data on our servers.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores all habit logs, preferences, AI interactions, and settings locally on your device. This information is not sent to us and remains under your control.

1.2 Data Collected by Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect certain data for ad delivery and fraud prevention. This may include:

  • Advertising ID (for example: AAID)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, OS version, app version)
  • App interactions with ads

This data is processed by Google according to their policies. We do not access, collect, or store this information ourselves.

You can review Google’s partner and advertising policies here:
Google Partner Policy

2. How We Use Information

  • To display ads using AdMob
  • To improve app performance (basic device logs)
  • To provide the habit-tracking features locally

We do NOT sell your data or share any habit information with third parties.

3. Permissions

The App may request only essential permissions such as local storage access for saving your data.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. Google AdMob automatically limits personalized ads for accounts identified as children.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • All habit data is stored locally and remains until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • AdMob retains advertising-related data according to Google’s policies.

6. Security

Since we do not store your data externally, security depends on your device’s security settings (such as device password, encryption, and backup configuration).

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect new app features or Google Play requirements. Updates will appear on this page with a new “Last Updated” date.
