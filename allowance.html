NSS Allowance Updates

Everything you need to know about monthly allowance amounts, payment schedule, arrears, and official announcements.

Last updated: 9 Nov 2025

Current Allowance Rate

The officially confirmed monthly allowance remains:

GH¢ 715.57 in effect since Jan 2023

This was adjusted upward by ~28% from GH¢559.04 in July 2023 and applied retroactively to January 2023.

Official NSS Site

Latest Updates (2024 → November 2025)

Nov 6 2025: The National Service Authority (NSA) acknowledged delays in the September 2025 allowance payment and assured that all required documentation has been submitted to the Office of the Accountant-General for final approval.

The Directorate of Finance and Administration confirmed that all processes have been completed, awaiting authorisation for fund release. Payment will be effected immediately upon approval.

Oct 2025: NSA indicated allowances were under review for a possible upward adjustment (not yet implemented as of Nov 2025). Pending
Apr 2025: NSA leadership assured personnel that the GH¢ 715 rate will be increased to a “more sustainable” amount soon. Assurance
Feb 2025: NSS confirmed payment of outstanding arrears to personnel beginning mid-February 2025. Arrears
Jan – Jul 2024: NSS confirmed payment of January 2024 allowances; processes underway to clear remaining arrears via e-Zwich. E-Zwich

How Allowance Payments Work

  • Monthly validation: Districts validate attendance/forms mid-month (around 16 – 20).
  • Approval & verification: NSA approval typically occurs between the 21st – 25th, followed by verification (~26 – 30).
  • Disbursement: Allowances are paid through e-Zwich cards linked to each NSS number.
  • Eligibility: Only validated personnel are paid for the corresponding service month.

Tip: If you miss validation, contact your district office promptly to correct records.
